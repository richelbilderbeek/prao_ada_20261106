# Reflection from supervisor

## What I did well

I treated Ada properly as a colleague.

## What I could do better

I am unsure when to postpone talking about the many things
about this place. It was only at 9:45 when she could get started.

I am unsure when to help how much. Trying yourself and getting stuck
is a big part of the work. Not getting things done due to this
is a regular day at the job too. Hence, it is realistic that
I do not help so much.

## My conclusion about my PRAO

I have seen Ada try her best.
She was focused, serious and did not give up.
She is friendly, polite and asked interesting questions.

She could have done an even better job, would she not have been 3 minutes late.
I do not completely blame her, as I cannot rule out the Swedish culture
in this 'lagom' behavior.

I wish I had been able to see more of her skills: problems with login,
laptop, HPC, etc are part of the job, but there was little left to go on.
This is not her fault, more something that would help me get a better
picture of her skill.

In the end, I think you cannot go wrong with Ada
and I can encourage any employee to offer her a PRAO/internship.
